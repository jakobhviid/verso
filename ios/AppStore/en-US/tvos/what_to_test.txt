- Manage Photos: Settings, Manage Photos, Turn On with iPhone; on the iPhone, Allow and Face ID. Then press and hold a photo, in a grid or full screen: Favorite, Archive, Move to Locked Folder, Delete. Turn Off on the TV, or Read Only on the iPhone (Account, Apple TV), takes it away.
- Settings: laid out as the Apple TV's own Settings, with the Top Shelf, the Locked Folder, Manage Photos, your account and About.
- Videos: the remote scrubs, shows the info panel, and a click on its edge steps to the next photo.
- The viewer: photos slide as you step, open out of their tile and close into it; a small Live Photo sign in the corner; HDR stays on once a video turned it on.
- The Locked Folder shows its photos; Lock on the iPhone closes it on the TV within a minute, and the TV stays signed in.
