- Videos: a video shows a play sign; click it to open the Apple TV's own player, with scrubbing, the transport bar and its audio and subtitle buttons. Menu goes back, a step at a time, to the viewer, where left and right go on.
- Photos grow out of their tile as they open, and shrink back into it as they close. The tile under the focus has a thin white outline.
