- The Locked Folder: in Settings, turn on Show Locked Folder in Collections, open it and choose Unlock with iPhone, then your iPhone. On the iPhone, allow it with Face ID or your PIN. Leaving the Locked Folder, or going to the Home Screen, locks it; the next visit asks the iPhone again. Lock on the iPhone (Account, Apple TV) locks it on the TV within a minute.
- The Top Shelf: in Settings, Top Shelf, choose its rows: Memories, Recent, Albums, Favorites and People. Albums come with the newest photos first, as Albums shows them. With Verso in the Home Screen's top row, a click on an item opens that memory, photo, album or person.
