- Setting up an Apple TV from its notification: on the TV choose Set Up with iPhone and your iPhone, then tap the notification on the iPhone; Verso opens to Set Up “name”? from wherever it was. Setting up from Account, Apple TV works as before.
- The account's picture stands at the top right of every tab on the iPhone (Library, the tabs you chose, Collections, Search), with its notifications' badge; on the iPad it stays in Collections.
- The Backup page: a section for each subject, what needs you first. The bar's parts are rows you can open, Left Out has a page of its own, and Free Up Space is a row opening its page.
- The Apple TV page (Account, Apple TV): the Apple TVs you set up, each with Forget, which asks first and shows when it is done.
